About

I’m in the kitchen using all 5 senses.

I’m finding people who know what I don’t, care about what they do,

and can't wait to tell me I’m wrong.

Biology is becoming something we can write to. What we do with it will outlive us. I want to build what comes next, with people who care about getting it right.
